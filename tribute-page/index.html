Maryam Mirzakhani

The first female mathematician to win the Fields Medal

Celebrating scientific exploration, mathematics, and the legacy of brilliant minds.

Here's a time line of Maryam Mirzakhani's life:

  • 1977 - Born in Tehran, Iran.
  • 1994 - Won a gold medal at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) in Hong Kong, the first Iranian female student to do so.
  • 1999 - Earned her Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from Sharif University of Technology.
  • 2004 - Earned her Ph.D. from Harvard University, under the supervision of the Fields Medalist Curtis McMullen.
  • 2009 - Became a professor at Stanford University.
  • 2014 - Awarded the Fields Medal, becoming the first woman and the first Iranian to receive the highest honor in mathematics.
  • 2017 - Passed away at the age of 40, leaving an everlasting legacy in mathematics.
